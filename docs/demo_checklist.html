Open http://localhost:5173, not http://127.0.0.1:5173. Vite binds the IPv6 loopback only, so the 127.0.0.1 spelling refuses the connection and you get a blank browser error page with both servers running perfectly. (Use npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1 if you want that spelling to work.) The frontend hard-defaults to http://localhost:8000 for the API, so the backend must be on port 8000. No .env is needed.

The checklist

Eleven steps, ~12 minutes at a talking pace. The timings are the step itself, not the talking. Tick state is saved in this browser, so hit Reset ticks before the real run.

  1. http://localhost:8000/api/health ~15 s

    Expect core_artifacts_available: true and nested_artifacts_dir: null. Then open the app — top-right chip must read “API ready” (green).

  2. /dataset ~60 s

    Four cards: 5,078,345 transactions · 30,470 banks · 370 campaigns · 258 evaluable, with “3,209 transactions” and “152 can be fragmented”. The transactions-per-day chart shows a dashed Cutoff line with the tail after it shaded.

  3. /campaigns — filter, then clear ~60 s

    25 rows by default. Set Pattern = BIPARTITE and Split = Test: removable chips appear and the live count drops. Clear both. rows open on click or Enter

  4. /campaigns/211 — the hub control ~90 s

    A clean star with the hub account dead centre. Sidebar: Group Hub. Now set View = Target 25% visibility — the badge must stay 100% / bin 100% and show the note “never reassigned (hub-shaped or too small)”. This is the control-group proof: one bank always sees everything.

  5. /campaigns/241 — the structural blind spot ~90 s

    Renders as 13 separate sender→receiver pairs, nothing joined to anything else. Sidebar: Evaluated Yes, Finished before cutoff Yes. Say: the matching rule needs 3 connected accounts; this campaign never connects more than 2, so the oracle misses it at both lookbacks — which is why oracle_reachable exists.

  6. Still on /campaigns/241 — step the View control ~60 s

    Target 100 → 75 → 50 → 25 gives badges 100% (bin 100%) → 77% (bin 75%) → 46% (bin 50%) → 23% (bin Low). The whole dose–response in one control.

  7. Still on /campaigns/241 — bank’s-eye view ~60 s

    Click bank tab I0, then one or two others. The header reads “This bank sees N of 13 transactions” and everything that bank cannot see fades out. At target 25% the best bank sees 3 of 13.

  8. /campaigns/219 — time replay ~75 s

    Press Play: transactions appear one at a time, the counter runs “Showing k of 7”, and it stops at the end. Press Show all. Drag a node, then press Reset view. The deadline and cutoff marks sit on the time strip below.

  9. /visibility — the toy, then the real data ~90 s

    Drag or click accounts between Bank I0 / I1 / I2 and watch the percentage recompute live. Move at least two accounts to two different banks before you expect the number to drop — one account moved out of a six-account ring still leaves bank I0 touching every transaction, so it correctly stays at 100% and looks stuck. Moving C and D to I1 and E and F to I2 walks it 100% → 83% → 67% → 50% (bin 50%), “3 of 6 transactions”. Then the real figures below: 152 fragmented · 219 landed in bin 100 · 2 unfragmentable. This is the only number on the whole site the frontend computes itself — and only for the scenario you just invented.

  10. /live — the live panel ~90 s

    Nothing to click. The panel opens on 2022-09-08 06:00:00 / I4 — the first setting where the two modes disagree, picked from the bundle itself — and scores it both ways at once. Ready in about a second. Check the two dropdowns read that before you start talking.

    missed → found is the whole demo. Point at that row. Below the table each column lists its own clusters, matched one first, with the rest behind “Show all N clusters”; each prints its own recompute time, around 150–350 ms.
    If you want to show it live rather than on open, change the Detection window dropdown and watch both columns re-score, or click the second entry under “Settings where the two modes differ” (2022-09-08 12:00:00, I4).

    Wandering off is safe. On any setting the bundle does not list, a note appears above the comparison — “at this setting the two modes agree on the outcome — neither finds the campaign. That is the common case: neither does in 58 of 64 settings offered here” — with a one-click Back to the setting where they differ. That note is a good thing to land on deliberately: it is the honest frame for the whole panel.

    Say this aloud, before anyone asks it.

    “Across the 64 settings this panel offers, neither mode finds the campaign in 58. Both find it in 4. Averaging alone finds it in 2, and the bank’s own model alone in 0 — so the two modes differ in two settings, and the own model never wins. This is a single window at a time; the aggregate is what the full sweep measures, and that sweep is still running.”

    The counts are on screen, in the Input card under “Settings where the two modes differ”. Reading them out first is what stops the panel being heard as “averaging always helps”.

    Two things to say out loud on this page. (1) Every number here is computed from a bundle built on the 1% sample, so it is a development check, not a result — the page says so at the top, but it says “if”, and the answer is yes. (1b) The selection rule, the rank-0-of-56 provenance and the “how to read this” notes are all still on the page, folded into the grey “How this campaign was selected” and “How to read this” disclosures. Open one if a faculty member asks how the campaign was picked — the answer is there, not lost. (2) The “And how often does it hold?” card at the bottom is a deliberate empty state, fixed 2026-11-16: it reads “The full-scale comparison has not been measured yet — the Phase 4 sweep is still running.” That is the honest answer and it is safe to read out. It is the same posture as the Results page. The development comparison that does exist is deliberately never shown here, because 1%-sample differences under that heading would read as a result.
  11. /results, then /methodology, then the theme toggle ~60 s

    Results: “Experiment results are not available yet”, the four conditions legend, five outlined figures, and no numbers at all. Methodology: 10 definition cards, each with its reason and values. Finally toggle light/dark (top right) while on campaign 241 and confirm the graph stays legible.

Say this, not that

Do not claim a CYCLE “looks like a ring”. The circle layout orders nodes alphabetically by account id, not by cycle traversal, so most edges cut across the middle as chords rather than following the rim (campaign 219: only 5 of 7 edges join circle-neighbours).

Say: “The layout is deterministic, so the same campaign draws identically every time — which is what we need for figures in the paper. The hub shapes in particular are unmistakable.”

Don’t say: “You can see at a glance that this is a ring.”

Lead the topology story with 211 (a genuinely perfect star) and 241 (13 visibly isolated pairs). Both carry the point without needing the ring.

If a faculty member reads a number off the screen, this is where each one comes from.

Home, Dataset, Campaigns, Campaign detail and the lower half of Visibility Lab are all served from backend/artifacts/, which was produced from the full IBM HI-Small dataset on Kaggle (5,078,345 transactions). Those are real figures.

The top half of Visibility Lab is the only number the frontend computes itself, and only for the scenario the viewer just invented.

Every number on /live is from the 1% sample — 53,960 transactions, not 5 million. Say “development check”, never “result”. The Results page and the live panel’s aggregate card are both deliberately empty because the full Phase 4 sweep has not finished; nothing on screen is a Phase 4 result, and no sample-derived aggregate is displayed anywhere.

If something goes wrong

SymptomCause and what to do
Chip reads “API unreachable”; pages say “Could not reach the API” Backend is down or not on port 8000. Restart Terminal 1. Pages recover on their own — no reload needed.
/api/health says artifacts are missing Read its hint field: it detects artifacts extracted one folder too deep. Fix the folder, then restart the server — artifacts are cached at startup.
A campaign page shows “Could not load this view” That id does not exist (valid ids are 0–369). Expected behaviour, not a crash.
Chip stuck on “Checking API”, pages stuck on loading This is the 2026-11-15 failure. A stale backend is holding port 8000 and answering nothing. It now resolves itself to “API unreachable” after 20 s. Fix: netstat -ano | findstr ":8000", taskkill /PID <pid> /T /F, restart Terminal 1. No browser reload needed — the pages recover on their own.
Browser shows “can’t reach this page” at the app itself You typed 127.0.0.1:5173. Vite listens on the IPv6 loopback only. Use http://localhost:5173.
Live panel: “No aggregate figures yet” at the bottom Expected and deliberate. The card explains itself: the full-scale comparison has not been measured yet. Everything above it on the page is live and correct. by design
Live panel: the two columns read the same You have moved off the opening setting, and the page says so: a note above the comparison reads “at this setting the two modes agree on the outcome” with the count behind it, and carries a one-click Back to the setting where they differ. Reloading /live also returns to it.
Live panel: a column says “Scoring...” for more than a few seconds Both modes are scored on every change, so the first load does two runs. If it does not settle, the backend is wedged: step 0.
A campaign URL with a non-numeric id, e.g. /campaigns/abc Now says “Not a campaign id” with a link back to the list. fixed 2026-11-16
Results page says “Could not check for results” Backend unreachable. It is deliberately not claiming results are missing — only that it cannot tell.
